🔰 Basic Symbols
• ^ – Start of line
Halimbawa: ^a → nagsisimula sa a

• $ – End of line
Halimbawa: ion$ → nagtatapos sa ion

• . – Any single character
Halimbawa: ^...$ → eksaktong 3 characters

• [] – Character set
Halimbawa: ^[a-m] → nagsisimula sa letter a to m

• | – OR operator
Halimbawa: a|b → a o b

🔁 Quantifiers
• + – 1 or more
Halimbawa: a+b+ → 1 or more a, followed by 1 or more b

• * – 0 or more (optional)
Halimbawa: a* → kahit walang a, okay lang

• ? – 0 or 1 only
Halimbawa: a? → pwede wala or isang a lang

• .* – anything (kahit ilang characters)
Halimbawa: M.* → nagsisimula sa M, then anything after

🧩 Grouping and References
• () – Group
Halimbawa: ^(...)(...)$ → dalawang group ng tig-3 characters

• $1, $2, etc. – Reference sa group
Halimbawa: Replace with $2$1 → pagbaliktarin yung dalawang group

🧪 Sample Pattern Explanation
• .*k(um|in)?ain.*
✅ Match sa anumang string na may:
• kahit ano sa simula (.*)
• tapos may k
• optionally may um OR in ((um|in)?)
• tapos may ain
• tapos kahit ano ulit sa dulo (.*)

🧵 Other Sample
• M...? +.*
✅ Match kung:
• M, then any 3 characters (...?)
• at least one space (_+ or +)
• tapos anything after (.*)
